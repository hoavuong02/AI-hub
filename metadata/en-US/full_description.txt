AI Hub is an app that lets you use many AI tools in one place. No more switching between apps!

Key features:
- Easy to use: Simple tabs to switch quickly
- Modern design: Uses Material Design 3
- Remembers last AI: Opens the AI you used last time
- Control your AIs: Choose which ones to show or disable any you don’t want
- Can backup & restore cookies: Save your logins and restore them anytime
- Auto theme change: Switches to light or dark mode automatically
- Dynamic colors: Beautiful colors on Android 12 and newer

Perfect for people who use many AI tools every day. Fully open-source and free.
